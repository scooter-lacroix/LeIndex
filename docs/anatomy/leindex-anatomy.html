The axis

One node-level corpus, banded into 8 phase stations from scan to checkpoint. Every station turns on the same 581-file inventory.

The gates

Three obsidian laminations: cpu, cuda, migraphx. A closed gate means that provider owns the neural layer; the others stay shut.

The tailstock

The precision layer. It dials in only when a language indexer is present; otherwise it retracts and the tier-0 graph answers.

The rosette

Eight teeth, one per index phase, lit in order. The job is checkpointed: pull the plug and it resumes on the lit tooth.

Five routers

20 handlers are advertised as 5 routers, one discriminator each, so a model picks an operation instead of a synonym.

The ledger

8 manifest layers, 458 feature-gate sites across 64 files, 17 language parsers.

The object is the explanation. LeIndex is one work axis that eight stations take turns cutting: scan, parse, graph, lexical, publish, neural, publish, checkpoint. The gates over the bed are the three execution providers — exactly one is open, and the neural station only cuts when one is. The tailstock is precision: it advances only when an external indexer is on the bench, and the tier-0 graph carries on without it. Nothing here is decorative: the band count is the phase count, the tooth count is the phase count, the gate count is the provider count, and the ruler has a tick per twelve files of the 581-file inventory.
